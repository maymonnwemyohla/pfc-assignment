# Assignment 01: What is Computer Science?

Welcome to your first assignment!

In the first lesson, we explored the foundational concept of computing: **Input – Process – Output (IPO)**. To solidify your understanding of how systems work to solve problems, please complete the following exercise.

---

## 🎯 Task

You are required to analyze systems based on the IPO model. Your analysis must include:
1. **Problem:** What is the user's pain point or goal? Why do they need this system?
2. **Input:** What data, materials, or actions must the user provide to the system?
3. **Process:** How does the system handle or transform those inputs? (Explain step-by-step).
4. **Output:** What is the final result produced by the system?

You will apply this to **two** different scenarios:
*   **Task 1:** A Real-world Analogy (e.g., making coffee, washing clothes).
*   **Task 2:** A Digital App/Software Analogy (e.g., Foodpanda, Facebook, Grab).
*   **Task 3:** A short reflection question on the definition of Computer Science.
