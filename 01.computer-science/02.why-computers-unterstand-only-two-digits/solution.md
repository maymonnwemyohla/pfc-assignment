### Task 1: Concept Questions

#### (a) Computers and the Binary System
* **Answer:**
  > Computers work using tiny switches that turn on and off. On means 1 and off means 0.This is called binary.

#### (b) Bit and Byte
* **Bit means:** A bit is the smallest unit of data in a computer.It holds a single binary value, either o or 1.
* **Byte means:** A byte is a group of 8 bits.It can store enough data to represent a single text character, like the letter "A".
* **Number of Bits in 1 Byte:** 8 Bits

### Task 2: Binary Combinations ($2^n$ Formula)

#### (a) Combinations for 4 Bits
* **Formula:** $2^4$
* **Answer:** 16 combinations

#### (b) Combinations and Range for 1 Byte (8 Bits)
* **Total Combinations ($2^8$):** 256 combinations
* **Representable Range:** From 0 to 255

### Task 3: Finger Binary Counting

#### (a) Decimal Value of Binary `00101`
* **Raised Fingers:** Middle (4) + Thumb (1)
* **Answer:** `5`

#### (b) Decimal Value of Binary `10011`
* **Raised Fingers:** Thumb (1) + Index (2) + Pinky (16)
* **Answer:** `19`

#### (c) Converting Decimal `13` to 5-bit Binary
* **Fingers to Raise:** Thumb (1) + Middle (4) + Ring (8)
* **5-bit Binary Code:** `01101`
